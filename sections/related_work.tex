\section{Related Work}

Previous work has explored LLM deployment on GPUs, TPUs, and custom accelerators. Systems such as TensorRT and XLA aim to optimize execution by compiling models to hardware-specific representations. Mamba-series models~\citep{gu2023mamba,dao2024transformers} have been shown to match transformer quality at reduced inference cost on GPUs, but their recurrent decode structure---a tight loop of small fused ops over a persistent state tensor---is particularly sensitive to dispatch overhead on PCIe-attached accelerators. Expert-parallel inference for sparse MoE models has been studied for GPU clusters~\citep{lepikhin2021gshard}, but the interaction between collective operations and device-side trace replay has not been characterised for Wormhole-class hardware.
